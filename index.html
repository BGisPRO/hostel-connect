How HBTU RidePool Works

Simple, reliable campus carpooling designed specifically for university students.

1

Choose Pickup & Destination

Select your campus hostel (e.g., Ramanujan or Alaknanda) and destination via dropdown or interactive OpenStreetMap.

2

Find a Pool or Post Your Own

Browse batchmates heading to Kanpur Central or Lucknow Airport, or create a pool specifying seats and estimated fare.

3

Coordinate & Confirm

Request a seat. The ride creator confirms passengers, and everyone coordinates pickup via in-pool chat.

4

1-Tap Cab App If Needed

If hiring an actual commercial cab, use our 1-tap deep links to launch Uber, Ola, or Rapido with coordinates already pre-filled.

Complete Storyboard Transcript:
1. Student selects verified hostel on campus (e.g. Ramanujan or Alaknanda).
2. Destination is chosen using search or interactive map (e.g. Kanpur Central or Lucknow Airport).
3. Matching active student pools appear with departure times and fare per student.
4. Student requests a seat; the pool creator accepts or rejects with a notification.
5. Once accepted, students settle the transparent cost share via UPI QR or cash.
6. If a commercial taxi is booked, 1-tap Uber/Ola/Rapido deep links transfer exact coordinates.

Campus Trust & Safety Guidelines

Built with verified student identities, strict privacy controls, and direct campus emergency access.

🎓

Verified Student Profiles

Accounts are verified with institutional university emails and academic roll numbers. Trust badges indicate student community standing.

🛡️

Girls-Only Pool Protection

Female students have dedicated filters to participate in pools strictly reserved for women riders, especially for late evening arrivals.

🔒

Default Privacy Protection

Personal phone numbers and payment IDs are kept completely private. Contact info is only shared with confirmed co-passengers in an approved ride.

🚨

Direct Campus Helplines

Immediate 1-tap call access to the HBTU Proctorial Board, East/West Security, UP Women Power Line (1090), and Police (112).

Status / Category What It Means Booking & Confirmation Action
Student Carpool Informal peer-to-peer ride coordination organized directly between HBTU students. Host confirms seat requests within HBTU RidePool. No commercial vehicle is booked.
Commercial Cab (Uber / Ola / Rapido) Third-party licensed commercial cab service ordered via official apps. Must be confirmed and paid inside Uber, Ola, or Rapido apps. HBTU RidePool only passes GPS coordinates.
Campus Auto Shuttle Shared local electric rickshaws and autos operating between East and West campus gates. Boarded in-person at campus gates. Tariffs are paid directly to auto operators.
